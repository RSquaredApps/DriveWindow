Privacy Policy

RSquared Apps respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how DriveWindow Route Tracker handles information when you use the iOS mobile application.

Summary

DriveWindow Route Tracker is designed primarily as an on-device route planning, route monitoring, and traffic alert tool. RSquared Apps does not sell, rent, or trade user personal data. The app is intended to use Apple system services and locally stored app data to provide route-related functionality.

Information You Enter or Save

DriveWindow may store information locally on your device so the app can function. This may include:

  • Saved route names, starting points, destinations, route order, favorites, and route preferences
  • Saved routes, alert thresholds, route status, and notification settings
  • Calculated route details such as estimated travel time, distance, delay, traffic severity, arrival estimates, and recent route status
  • Saved arrival plans and recommended departure times
  • Preferences such as navigation app selection, route stop categories, route display settings, and Live Activity pinning

This information is used to operate DriveWindow. Unless a future version clearly states otherwise, RSquared Apps does not receive or maintain this saved route information on its own servers.

Location Information

DriveWindow may request location access when you choose to create or monitor a route that starts from your current location, when you ask the app to calculate directions, or when route-related features require a location to function.

  • Location access is used to provide route monitoring, route previews, current-location starting points, ETA calculations, and navigation-related features.
  • DriveWindow may request “Always” location access when necessary to keep current-location routes accurate during an active monitoring window.
  • You may deny, limit, or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • If location access is denied or limited, some current-location, monitoring, or route-refresh features may not work as intended.

Those third-party services may process information necessary to provide their services, such as route endpoints, map regions, approximate or precise location, search terms, device network information, and related request metadata. RSquared Apps does not control Apple’s or other third-party providers’ independent data practices. Review the privacy policies and settings for Apple and any applicable third-party service providers for more information.

Notifications, Live Activities, Widgets, Apple Watch, and Future Platform Support

DriveWindow may use local notifications, Live Activities, widgets, and Apple Watch sync to display route status, ETA, delay, traffic trend, weather summary, distance, alert requirements, and similar route information. CarPlay is not currently available.

  • Notification permission is optional and controlled through iOS Settings.
  • Live Activities and widgets may display route information on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, Home Screen, or other system surfaces depending on your device settings.
  • Apple Watch experiences may display route data from DriveWindow in connected Apple ecosystem contexts. CarPlay is not currently available; any future CarPlay support may be subject to Apple approval, platform requirements, and technical availability.
