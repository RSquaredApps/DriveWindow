Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

DriveWindow: Traffic & Weather Tracker is built to help you plan safer, smarter drives while keeping your route data simple and understandable.

App DriveWindow Developer RSquared Apps Effective Date May 31, 2026

Summary

DriveWindow is designed primarily as an on-device route planning, route monitoring, traffic alert, and route weather tool. RSquared Apps does not sell, rent, or trade user personal data. The app uses saved route information and Apple system services to provide traffic, ETA, location, notification, Live Activity, widget, and Apple Watch features.

Information you enter or save

DriveWindow may store information locally on your device so the app can function, including:

  • Saved route names, starting points, destinations, route order, favorites, and route preferences.
  • Alert thresholds, notification settings, route status, and monitoring choices.
  • Calculated route details such as estimated travel time, distance, delay, traffic severity, arrival estimates, and recent route status.
  • Saved arrival plans, recommended departure times, navigation app selection, stop categories, display settings, and Live Activity pinning.

Unless a future version clearly states otherwise, RSquared Apps does not receive or maintain saved route information on its own servers.

Location information

DriveWindow may request location access when you create or monitor a route that starts from your current location, calculate directions, preview route weather, search for stops, or use route-related features.

  • Location is used for route monitoring, current-location starts, ETA calculations, route previews, and navigation-related features.
  • The app may request Always location access to keep current-location routes accurate during an active monitoring window.
  • You can deny, limit, or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings.

Maps, traffic, weather, and third-party services

DriveWindow may rely on Apple frameworks and system services, including MapKit, Core Location, local notifications, ActivityKit, WidgetKit, and Watch connectivity. Route, traffic, ETA, directions, weather radar, map tile, and place-search features may require requests to Apple or other selected data providers.

These services may process information necessary to provide their services, such as route endpoints, map regions, approximate or precise location, search terms, device network information, and related request metadata. RSquared Apps does not control Apple’s or other providers’ independent data practices.

Notifications, Live Activities, widgets, and Apple Watch

If enabled, DriveWindow may use local notifications, Live Activities, widgets, and Apple Watch sync to display route status, ETA, delay, traffic trend, weather summary, distance, alert requirements, and similar route information.

  • Notification permission is optional and controlled through iOS Settings.
  • Live Activities and widgets may display route information on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, Home Screen, or other system surfaces.
  • Apple Watch experiences may display synced route data from DriveWindow.

No sale of personal data

DriveWindow is not designed to sell personal data, use advertising identifiers, or use user information for behavioral advertising.

Data retention

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete or change it in the app, delete the app, or erase/reset your device.

Your choices

You can delete routes, disable notifications, disable location, stop Live Activities, or delete the app through iOS and app settings.

Children’s privacy

DriveWindow is not directed to children under 13 years of age. RSquared Apps does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to RSquared Apps, contact RSquared Apps so appropriate steps can be taken.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

RSquared Apps may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

Frequently asked questions

Why does DriveWindow ask for location access?

Location access is used when you choose Current Location as a route start, calculate routes, monitor route status, and keep route information accurate while a monitoring window is active.

Why are ETA or traffic details unavailable?

ETA and traffic information can depend on location permission, network access, route availability, and Apple mapping services. Try refreshing the route, checking your connection, or confirming the route addresses.

How do alerts work?

When alerts are enabled, DriveWindow can notify you when route conditions match your selected monitoring preferences, including traffic changes or route status changes.

How do I stop a Live Activity?

Open DriveWindow and stop tracking or unpin the route. You can also manage Live Activities through iOS controls on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island.
